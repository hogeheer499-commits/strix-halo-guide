## How to use the comparison

Compare memory/SSD, complete-system cost, delivery, ports, cooling, firmware,
return eligibility and evidence depth. None of these unmatched results and
dated offers establishes a universal cheapest, best-value or best-support
winner. Dynamic pages can show generic unavailable banners alongside available
variant data; the selected SKU and final cart take precedence.

The deepest first-party evidence remains Beelink. GMKtec EVO-X2 has attributed
native Linux, WSL2, tuned external, ciru-ai and Kyanite evidence, with exact
memory/runtime differences preserved in the [system matrix](SYSTEM_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md).
There is **no first-party exact-retail-SKU GMKtec buyer-path campaign** here.
Community 96GB results do not qualify a current 128GB retail package, EVO-X3,
fresh installation, support quality or delivery. The missing proof is a stock
exact-SKU setup → text/image/tool/client → restart/reboot run with elapsed time,
interventions, firmware and model hashes.
