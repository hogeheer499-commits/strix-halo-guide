Diagnose known ROCm/PyTorch/llama.cpp failure modes against a closed catalog

Usage: rocm diagnose [OPTIONS]

Options:
      --symptom <SYMPTOM>  Raw error text from the user; sharpens keyword scoring
      --top <TOP>          Show at most this many matches (default 5) [default: 5]
      --json               Emit the machine-readable diagnosis JSON
  -h, --help               Print help
