# Buyer Use Cases

This page translates the technical guide into practical buyer decisions. It is not generic marketing, and it does not claim that every workflow is proven. The goal is to help buyers decide whether AMD Strix Halo / Ryzen AI MAX+ local-AI hardware fits their real use before spending a large amount of money.

Confidence levels:

- Proven: supported by direct repo evidence for the stated scope.
- Partly proven: evidence exists, but the exact buyer workflow, OS, backend, model, or hardware variant still needs more validation.
- Needs more hardware: promising or important, but current evidence is not enough for a broad recommendation.

## Buyer Value

The guide helps buyers reduce risk before purchase:

- It identifies known-good setup paths.
- It separates easy setup from fastest measured paths.
- It links claims to raw evidence instead of relying on screenshots or vague posts.
- It preserves failed and experimental routes so buyers do not waste time on unsupported assumptions.
- It keeps first-party Beelink results separate from community-submitted Corsair, GMKtec, MS-S1-Max, Nimo, RPC, and power results.
