# Exact-configuration storefront snapshot — observations of September 30, 2026

Observed on **2026-09-30** (UTC times are given where they were recorded) from
official storefronts, price-comparison sites and vendor pages; the file is dated
2026-10-01. These are seller-advertised offers and listings, not purchases,
checkout totals, guaranteed stock or measured support outcomes. Every row names
its country or site, the currency and the tax status where known: "tax not
stated" means the page showed no tax information. Unless stated otherwise, US
stores are in USD and EU stores in EUR. Prices can differ by visitor location;
the EU-store observations were made from a Netherlands IP address. Shipping and
import totals were not established; verify the destination-specific cart.

## How to use the comparison

Compare memory/SSD, complete-system cost, delivery, ports, cooling, firmware,
return eligibility, seller and warranty terms (including commercial use) and
evidence depth. None of these unmatched results and dated offers establishes a
universal cheapest, best-value or best-support winner. Dynamic pages can show
generic unavailable banners alongside available variant data; the selected SKU
and final cart take precedence. Prices that carry a promotion end date, a batch
limit or a pre-order label are not comparable with list prices of systems that
ship now.

The deepest first-party evidence remains Beelink. GMKtec EVO-X2 has attributed
native Linux, WSL2, tuned external, ciru-ai and Kyanite evidence, with exact
memory/runtime differences preserved in the [system matrix](SYSTEM_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md).
There is **no first-party exact-retail-SKU buyer-path campaign** for any system
listed here, and no 192GB or PRO 495 system is measured. The first-party
Beelink GTR9 Pro was bought by the maintainer (see [disclosure](VENDOR_DISCLOSURE.md)).
