# Professional Services

The public guide remains free. These paid scopes are for people and teams who
want hands-on help, a controlled reproduction, or evidence for a specific
system.

All work remains evidence-led. Payment does not buy a positive conclusion,
hidden edits, or removal of accurate negative results.

## Setup Rescue

**Introductory fixed price: EUR 199**

For an owner who has a Strix Halo system but is stuck between BIOS settings,
Linux, GPU access, Ollama, `llama.cpp`, model formats, or quantization choices.

The scope includes:

- Pre-session review of one machine, one operating system, and one primary
  local-AI goal.
- One remote session of up to 90 minutes.
- A known-good path or a clearly documented blocker.
- A short written follow-up with the commands, versions, and next steps used.

It does not include an operating-system reinstall, hardware repair, data
recovery, unattended administration, or a guaranteed performance number.

## Independent Reproduction Sprint

**Starting price: EUR 950**

For a model publisher, runtime team, developer, reviewer, or vendor that wants
an independent Strix Halo reproduction of one clearly scoped claim or route.

A normal sprint includes:

- One agreed model or artifact, backend, and primary question.
- A written test plan before execution.
- Exact hardware, software, command, model, and quantization metadata.
- Cold or warm controls where they materially affect the result.
- Repeats, raw logs, structured results, caveats, and failed-path notes.
- A concise findings report with claim-to-evidence links.

Public publication is optional and must be agreed before the work starts. If a
paid result becomes public, the relationship and scope are disclosed. Private
results do not become public benchmark claims without written permission and a
separate evidence review.

## OEM Buyer-Path Pilot

**Starting price: EUR 2,500 plus access to the target system**

For an OEM, system vendor, reviewer, or developer-relations team that wants a
reproducible path from a retail AI PC to working local AI.

A normal pilot covers:

- One named system and operating-system path.
- BIOS, firmware, memory, driver, and runtime capture.
- Two agreed runtime paths and up to three named workloads.
- Setup interventions, failures, restart persistence, and capability checks.
- A public or private technical report, depending on the agreed scope.
- Raw evidence and factual review, without vendor editorial control over the
  conclusions.

The goal is to reduce buyer and support friction: make the system easier to
evaluate, configure, reproduce, review, and recommend.

## Custom Local-AI Proof Of Concept

Custom work can cover a private local model, API, retrieval workflow, coding
assistant, serving route, or deployment decision. These projects are quoted
after a short technical intake because hardware access, privacy, data,
licensing, and success criteria vary substantially.

- Client data and credentials remain the client's responsibility. Back up
  important data before any remote session.
- Vendors may correct factual errors. They do not receive editorial control

- Sponsored, loaned, gifted, affiliate, and early-access work is disclosed
