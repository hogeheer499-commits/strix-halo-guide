## Evidence fit and decision limits

The [buying guide](README.md#buying-guide) separates Beelink first-party evidence from Corsair/GMKtec/Nimo community bundles, Minisforum Windows/API evidence and thinner same-shape evidence for Framework, Bosgame and HP. These are not matched chassis rankings. Compare an exact complete configuration and return path against your actual model/context/task needs; neither a low storefront base price nor modular design proves better support or model quality.

No purchase, checkout, support request, warranty claim or new hardware test was performed for this snapshot. The earlier AMD Micro Center price/exclusivity/start-date and Framework 192GB PRO 495 statements lacked sufficient linked primary substantiation and are not used here.
